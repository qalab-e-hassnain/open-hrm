# Replace these

- **`logo.webp`** — a placeholder. Your logo, square, ideally 256×256 or
  larger. Shown in the sidebar, on the login screen, on ID cards and on
  generated documents. `public/logo.webp` is the same image and should be
  replaced too.
- **`login_background.webp`** — the login screen backdrop. Swap it for your
  own or remove the rule in `src/pages/auth/Login.css`.

Colours live in `src/index.css`: change `--brand-red` and the three shades
beside it, and the rest follows.
